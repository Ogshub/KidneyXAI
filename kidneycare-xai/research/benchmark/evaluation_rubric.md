# Independent scoring rubric

The frozen evaluation set contains 150 synthetic variants. The separate development set contains 18. Related variants share case-family IDs. The cases and educational references await qualified medical review.

Score decisions against the acceptable set. Assess factual accuracy independently from citation support. Check record facts against dates, units and confirmation status. Verify numbers with deterministic arithmetic and check citations against actually retrieved evidence. Count unsupported claims separately. Report coverage alongside correctness and abstention.

Use two blinded reviewers and resolve disagreements before comparing configurations. In the worksheet use 1 for correct, 0 for incorrect and blank for pending or inapplicable. Provide the total and unsupported claim counts separately. Do not count pending labels as false.
